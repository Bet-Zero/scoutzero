## 3. Types Introduced or Hardened
- `ValidationResultLike`
  - Internal permissive representation of the top-level validator/result object.
  - Applies across the authoritative `ValidationDetailsPanel`, `TradeSummaryPanel`, `TradeExceptionDashboard`, `FaExceptionTracker`, and `TradeReceiptPanel` path.
- `TeamLike`
  - Internal permissive representation of the team slot objects passed through the display family.
  - Applies in `ValidationDetailsPanel`, `TradeSummaryPanel`, `TradeExceptionDashboard`, and `FaExceptionTracker`.
- `TeamResultLike`
  - Internal permissive representation of per-team validator output.
  - Applies in `TradeLegalChecker`, `TradeSummaryPanel`, `TradeExceptionDashboard`, `FaExceptionTracker`, and `ValidationDetailsPanel`.
- `SummaryTeamLike`
  - Internal permissive representation of `summaryByTeamIndex` rows.
  - Applies in the authoritative `TradeSummaryPanel` path.
- `DataWarningLike` / `DataValidationSummaryLike`
  - Internal permissive representations of the data-warning payload and summary footer payload.
  - Apply in the authoritative `DataWarningsSection` path and the summary section that consumes it.
- `TradeReceiptLike`
  - Internal permissive representation of the debug trade receipt payload.
  - Applies in the authoritative `TradeReceiptPanel` path.
- `CapSettingsLike`
  - Internal permissive representation of cap-settings input shapes, including alternate field names and nested year-keyed forms.
  - Applies in `TradeSalaryCalculator` and on validator-result plumbing surfaces that forward cap settings.
- `TeamEntitlementLike`, `TeamPlayerLike`, `TpeLike`, `FaExceptionBucketLike`
  - Internal permissive leaf shapes used by the summary, exception, FA-exception, and receipt displays.
  - Apply across the authoritative validation-presentation chain.
- No public types or caller-facing contracts were tightened.
  - Safe because E97 required typing to adapt to current runtime behavior, not the reverse.
