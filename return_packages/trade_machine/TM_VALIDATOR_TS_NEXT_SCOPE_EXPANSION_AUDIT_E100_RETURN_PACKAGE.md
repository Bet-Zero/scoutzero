### Candidate 1 — Trade Team Card leaf family
- Scope name: `Trade Team Card leaf family`
- Lane: `batched low-risk`
- What it includes:
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/CapImpactTiles.jsx`
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/SelectTeamCard.jsx`
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/OutgoingPlayersList.jsx`
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/TradePlayerRow.jsx`
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/EntitlementPicksList.jsx`
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/EntitlementPickRow.jsx`
  - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/TradeExceptionManager.jsx`
- What it excludes:
  - excluded orchestration hubs:
    - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/TradeTeamCard.jsx`
    - `src/features/architect/tradeMachine/TradeEditor.jsx`
  - the already-closed E97 validator/result-presentation family
  - the already-closed E99 preview/export family
  - `src/features/architect/utils/mutationPipeline.js`
- Estimated live JS/JSX/TSX business-logic file count: `7`
- Per-file role and coupling assessment:
  - `CapImpactTiles.jsx` — `display-only`; it computes and renders cap/apron tiles from passed-in team, send, incoming, and snapshot props, but it does not own mutation state or menu actions
  - `SelectTeamCard.jsx` — `display-only`; it is a minimal team-select shell with only `onSelectTeam` and optional `onRemove`
  - `TradeExceptionManager.jsx` — `display-only`; it formats and displays current and expired exceptions from passed-in `exceptions` and `teamId`
  - `OutgoingPlayersList.jsx` — `row/list composition`; it sorts available players, coordinates the open-menu row state, and passes callbacks down to `TradePlayerRow`
  - `EntitlementPicksList.jsx` — `row/list composition`; it filters, dedupes, groups, coordinates menu state, and passes routing/edit/create callbacks down to `EntitlementPickRow`
  - `TradePlayerRow.jsx` — `action/menu/callback-heavy`; it owns the player action menu, sign-and-trade gating, contract-edit trigger, and trade/undo dispatch, but all behavior is still driven through props rather than hidden local orchestration
  - `EntitlementPickRow.jsx` — `action/menu/callback-heavy`; it owns entitlement menu actions, routing selection, edit/view/revert/delete dispatch, and pick-row projection display, but the trade state still comes from props
- Why it is a serious candidate:
  - It is still the strongest remaining coherent feature surface after E99.
  - All seven files remain directly tied to the excluded `TradeTeamCard.jsx` surface without crossing into the heavier orchestration logic in `TradeTeamCard.jsx` or `TradeEditor.jsx`.
  - The core family is substantial enough to matter at roughly `1474` LOC combined, but still bounded.
- Why it is a good next move:
  - The family boundary is cleaner than the remaining dashboard/support and shared/support batches.
  - The actual risk is concentrated in the two action-heavy row files rather than in hidden world-write orchestration.
  - Execution-time inspection did not show `TradePlayerRow.jsx`, `EntitlementPickRow.jsx`, or `TradeExceptionManager.jsx` to be so tightly coupled to excluded `TradeTeamCard.jsx` internals that the family boundary collapses.
- Why it is not blocker-free:
  - `TradePlayerRow.jsx` and `EntitlementPickRow.jsx` are the main risk points because they carry the menu/callback contract back into `TradeTeamCard.jsx`.
  - That coupling argues for careful migration, but not for rejecting the family or silently shrinking it.
