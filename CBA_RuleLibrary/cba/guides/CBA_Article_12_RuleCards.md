# Article XII – Option Clauses

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Team Options
**Purpose**: Used to determine when Teams may include option years in contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Contract type (Veteran, Rookie, Second Round Exception)
- Option year terms
  **Rules**:

1. Team Options only allowed if:

   - Specifically negotiated,
   - Extends contract by ≤1 year,
   - Exercisable only once,
   - Option year salary/bonuses ≥100% of prior year,
   - Other terms remain unchanged (except payment schedule).

2. Rookie Scale Contracts follow Article VIII instead.
   **Edge Cases**: Second Round Pick Exception contracts exempt from unchanged-terms rule.
   **Citation**: Article XII, Sec. 1

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Player Options
**Purpose**: Used to determine when Players may include option years in contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Contract type (Veteran, Rookie, Rookie Scale)
- Option year protections
  **Rules**:

1. Player Options only allowed if:

   - Specifically negotiated,
   - Extends contract by ≤1 year,
   - Exercisable only once,
   - Option year salary/bonuses ≥100% of prior year,
   - All other terms unchanged.

2. If protection applies, contract must include either:

   - Clause A (protection survives termination), or
   - Clause B (protection void if terminated pre-exercise).
   - Clause B options cannot be exercisable earlier than day after last game of prior season.

3. Early Termination Options (ETOs) allowed if:

   - Exercisable once,
   - Effective no earlier than after 4th season of contract (or extended rookie contract).
   - Cannot add ETOs mid-contract or in non-rookie extensions.
     **Edge Cases**: Rookie Scale Extensions may add ETO simultaneously if conditions met.
     **Citation**: Article XII, Sec. 2

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: No Conditional Options
**Purpose**: Used to prevent conditional or performance-based option triggers.
**Inputs**:

- Option/ETO clause
  **Rules**:

1. Option/ETO exercise rights must be fixed at contract signing.
2. ETO effective season must also be fixed at signing.
3. No contingent or individually-negotiated conditions allowed.
   **Edge Cases**: Applies to both Team and Player Options.
   **Citation**: Article XII, Sec. 3

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Option and ETO Exercise Periods
**Purpose**: Used to determine deadlines for exercising Options and ETOs.
**Inputs**:

- Option type (Team, Player, ETO)
- Player’s potential RFA status
  **Rules**:

1. ETO must be exercised by June 29, 5:00 p.m. ET before Effective Season.
2. Standard Options must be exercised by June 29, 5:00 p.m. ET before Option Season.
3. If player would become RFA if option not exercised → exercise deadline is June 25 before Option Season.
   **Edge Cases**: Different deadlines for RFAs ensure tender/QO process can proceed.
   **Citation**: Article XII, Sec. 4

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Option Exercise Notices
**Purpose**: Used to regulate league notification process for exercised or declined Options/ETOs.
**Inputs**:

- Team or player option/ETO decision
- Notice filed with NBA
  **Rules**:

1. NBA must notify Players Association within 2 business days of receiving exercise or non-exercise notice.
   **Edge Cases**: Applies equally to Team and Player Options and ETOs.
   **Citation**: Article XII, Sec. 5
