# Article VII – Salary Cap, Tax, Exceptions, and Extensions

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Salary Cap & Thresholds
**Purpose**: Used to establish annual Salary Cap, Minimum Team Salary, Tax Level, and Apron Levels.
**Inputs**:

- Salary Cap Year
- Previous year’s Salary Cap
- BRI calculation
  **Rules**:

1. Salary Cap set each year per BRI calculation.
2. Minimum Team Salary = 90% of Salary Cap.
3. Tax Level = 121.5% of Salary Cap.
4. First Apron: Tax Level + scaling formula based on 2023–24 baseline.
5. Second Apron: Tax Level + \$17.5M, scaled annually.
6. None of these values can decrease YoY or increase by more than 10%.
   **Edge Cases**: Expansion/lockout adjustments may apply.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 2(a)

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Salary Cap Operation
**Purpose**: Used to determine when a Team may exceed the Salary Cap.
**Inputs**:

- Team Salary
- Exception availability
  **Rules**:

1. Teams cannot exceed the Cap unless using an Exception.
2. Teams with “Room” may sign players up to their Room limit.
   **Edge Cases**: Renegotiation and Extension limits apply.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 2(b)

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Minimum Team Salary Operation
**Purpose**: Used to enforce Minimum Team Salary compliance and penalties.
**Inputs**:

- Team Salary (Cap Hold and Payment Salary)
- Minimum Team Salary threshold
  **Rules**:

1. If Payment Salary < Minimum Team Salary, team pays shortfall to NBA.
2. Team below Minimum Team Salary loses tax distribution eligibility.
3. Team Salary deemed increased by shortfall during season.
4. If Team Salary falls below threshold, must correct within 1 day.
   **Edge Cases**: May exceed Minimum Team Salary freely if under Cap + Exceptions.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 2(c)

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Luxury Tax Operation
**Purpose**: Used to determine tax owed by Teams above the Tax Level.
**Inputs**:

- Tax Team Salary
- Tax Level
- Repeater status (3 of 4 years above tax)
- Salary Cap (for Tax Bracket Amount)
  **Rules**:

1. Teams pay tax on amount above Tax Level.
2. Rates vary by bracket (\$5M increments).
3. Standard Tax Rates apply unless repeater trigger met, then Repeater Tax Rates apply.
4. Rates escalate per bracket and after 400% of Tax Bracket Amount.
   **Edge Cases**: Trade bonuses, suspensions, and certain contracts adjust Tax Salary calculation.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 2(d)

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Apron Operation & Transaction Restrictions
**Purpose**: Used to determine when Apron Levels restrict team transactions.
**Inputs**:

- Apron Team Salary
- Transaction type (Exception use, trade, signing)
- Applicable Apron Level
  **Rules**:

1. Apron Team Salary includes specific add-backs (bonuses, holds, tenders).
2. Teams cannot engage in listed transactions if resulting Apron Team Salary exceeds applicable Apron.
3. Transactions have tiered limits (First Apron vs Second Apron).
4. Special restrictions apply after using Taxpayer MLE.
   **Edge Cases**: 2023–24 has carve-outs for transactions exceeding Apron.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 2(e)

---

## Rule Card 6

**Name**: Draft Pick Penalty for Second Apron Teams
**Purpose**: Used to penalize teams consistently above Second Apron.
**Inputs**:

- Apron Team Salary relative to Second Apron
- Consecutive years above Second Apron
  **Rules**:

1. If Apron Salary > Second Apron, team is “Second Apron Team.”
2. Starting 2024–25, Second Apron Teams cannot trade 1st-round pick 7 years out.
3. If above Second Apron in 2 of next 4 years, that pick is moved to end of 1st round.
4. Multiple penalized teams ordered by inverse record.
   **Edge Cases**: Teams may regain trading rights if under Apron 3 of 4 following years.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 2(f)

---

## Rule Card 7

**Name**: Over-38 Rule
**Purpose**: Used to prevent salary backloading in contracts past age 38.
**Inputs**:

- Player age at signing
- Contract length (≥4 years)
  **Rules**:

1. Salaries after age-38 redistributed to prior years pro rata.
2. Exceptions for 35–36 year olds signing with Prior Team for 5 years.
3. Redistribution may be triggered earlier depending on “Zero Year.”
   **Edge Cases**: No reallocation for ≤4-year deals signed at 35–36.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 3(a)

---

## Rule Card 8

**Name**: Signing Bonuses Allocation
**Purpose**: Used to allocate signing bonuses across contract years.
**Inputs**:

- Bonus type (contract, trade, extension)
- Protected Base Compensation
- ETO presence
  **Rules**:

1. Signing bonuses allocated across years proportionate to protected salary.
2. If none protected, entire bonus applied to first year.
3. Extension bonuses allocated only to extended years; if none protected, frontloaded.
   **Edge Cases**: Cap rules differ if team is over vs under Cap at time of extension.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 3(b)

---

## Rule Card 9

**Name**: Incentive Compensation
**Purpose**: Used to classify bonuses as “likely” or “unlikely” for Salary inclusion.
**Inputs**:

- Player/Team prior year performance
- Bonus conditions
  **Rules**:

1. Bonus counts if it would have been earned based on prior year performance.
2. Disputes resolved by jointly appointed Expert.
3. Rookies or missed-year veterans: bonus counts if “likely to be earned.”
4. During disputes, Salary includes full disputed amount.
   **Edge Cases**: NBA/Union may challenge classification at signing.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 3(d)

---

## Rule Card 10

**Name**: International Player Payments
**Purpose**: Used to regulate buyout payments to overseas teams.
**Inputs**:

- Payment amount
- Excluded Payment Amount for given year
- Contract type (standard, Two-Way, Exhibit 10)
  **Rules**:

1. Payments above exclusion count as Salary (signing bonus).
2. Payments up to exclusion for release are not Salary.
3. Cannot use same exclusion >1 in 3 years for same player.
4. Teams barred from paying exclusions to sign Two-Way/Exhibit 10 players.
   **Edge Cases**: Excluded payment thresholds escalate annually.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 3(e)

---

## Rule Card 11

**Name**: Free Agent Amounts (Cap Holds)
**Purpose**: Used to calculate Team Salary for unsigned free agents.
**Inputs**:

- Free Agent type (Qualifying, Early Qualifying, Non-Qualifying)
- Prior Salary vs Estimated Average Player Salary
- Rookie Scale contract status
  **Rules**:

1. Qualifying FA hold = 150% of prior salary (≥Avg), 190% if \<Avg.
2. Rookie Scale FA (after 2nd option year) = 250% or 300%.
3. Early Qualifying = 130%.
4. Non-Qualifying = 120%.
5. Cannot exceed Max or fall below unreimbursed Minimum.
   **Edge Cases**: Two-Way FA = minimum hold. Rookie Scale exceptions apply.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 4(d)

---

## Rule Card 12

**Name**: First Round Pick Cap Holds
**Purpose**: Used to account for unsigned draft picks in Team Salary.
**Inputs**:

- Draft position (Rookie Scale Amount)
- Signing status (NBA vs non-NBA team)
- Team’s renunciation decision
  **Rules**:

1. Unsigned picks count at 120% of Rookie Scale.
2. Removed if player signs overseas or renounced; reinstated July 1.
3. Cap hold resets each year to updated Rookie Scale at same pick slot.
   **Edge Cases**: Team may elect to exclude with written renunciation.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 4(e)

---

## Rule Card 13

**Name**: Incomplete Roster Charges
**Purpose**: Used to ensure minimum 12-player roster for Cap accounting.
**Inputs**:

- Number of players under contract
- Minimum Salary for 0-year player
  **Rules**:
- If <12 players, add charge equal to difference × 0-year minimum salary.
  **Edge Cases**: Only applies July 1 to preseason.
  **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 4(f)

---

## Rule Card 14

**Name**: Long-Term Injury Salary Exclusion
**Purpose**: Used to exclude contracts of players with career-ending injury/illness.
**Inputs**:

- Medical determination
- One-year anniversary of last game played
  **Rules**:

1. Salary excluded beginning 1 year after last game.
2. Determination made by joint physician.
3. If player returns and plays ≥25 games, Salary reinstated.
   **Edge Cases**: Team cannot re-sign/re-acquire excluded player.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 4(h)

---

## Rule Card 15

**Name**: Salary Cap Exceptions (General)
**Purpose**: Used to determine when a team can exceed the Salary Cap.
**Inputs**:

- Exception type (MLE, Bi-Annual, Disabled Player, Traded Player, etc.)
  **Rules**:

1. Teams may exceed Cap using Exceptions.
2. Each Exception has limits on amount, term, and frequency.
3. Exceptions only usable if Team Salary ≥Cap or within Exception value.
4. Unused Exceptions (except TPE, Min, Disabled) reduce daily from Jan 10 onward.
   **Edge Cases**: Exceptions cannot be aggregated unless specified.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 6

---

## Rule Card 16

**Name**: Veteran Extensions
**Purpose**: Used to extend contracts for veteran players.
**Inputs**:

- Contract length
- Years since signing/renegotiation
- Salary % limits
  **Rules**:

1. 3–4 year contracts: extendable after 2 years.
2. 5–6 year contracts: extendable after 3 years.
3. Not extendable if shortened via ETO.
4. First-year extended salary capped at 140% of prior salary or Avg Player Salary.
5. Designated Vet Extensions allowed up to max salary.
   **Edge Cases**: Trade-related extensions have different % rules.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 7(a)

---

## Rule Card 17

**Name**: Rookie Scale Extensions
**Purpose**: Used to extend First Round Pick contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Rookie Scale contract status (after 3rd year)
- Extension window (after Moratorium, before Regular Season of 4th year)
  **Rules**:

1. Eligible to extend in 4th year window.
2. First-year salary capped at Rookie Scale max (Article II, Sec. 7).
   **Edge Cases**: Higher Max clauses allowed for award criteria.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 7(b)

---

## Rule Card 18

**Name**: Renegotiations
**Purpose**: Used to restructure contracts for current year salary.
**Inputs**:

- Contract length (≥4 years)
- Team Salary vs Cap (must be under)
- Timing (not Mar 1–Jun 30)
  **Rules**:

1. Eligible only after 3 years for ≥4 year contracts.
2. Cannot renegotiate if already extended or previously renegotiated within 3 years.
3. Increases must apply to all remaining years.
4. Only teams under Cap may renegotiate.
5. No decreases in salary/bonuses allowed.
   **Edge Cases**: Cannot renegotiate March 1–June 30.
   **Citation**: Article VII, Sec. 7(c–d)
