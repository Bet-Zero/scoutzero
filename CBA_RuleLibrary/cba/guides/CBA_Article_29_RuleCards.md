# Article XXIX – Rosters and Award Eligibility

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Active List Requirements
**Purpose**: Used to determine minimum and maximum number of players on Active List.
**Inputs**:

- Regular Season dates
- Season status (in-season vs offseason)
  **Rules**:

1. Teams must carry 12–15 players on Active List.
2. Exception: may carry 11 players for max 2 consecutive weeks and 28 total days.
3. Must have ≥8 players on bench for all Regular Season games.
4. Offseason (post-Season through start of next Season): up to 21 players allowed (including Two-Way).
5. Inactive and Two-Way players transferred to Active List after season ends.
   **Edge Cases**: 11-player roster limits reset after each exception period.
   **Citation**: Article XXIX, Sec. 1

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Roster Size Requirements
**Purpose**: Used to determine Active + Inactive List sizes during season.
**Inputs**:

- Regular Season dates
- Team hardship exception status
- Two-Way players on roster
  **Rules**:

1. Standard requirement: 14–15 players combined Active + Inactive List.
2. Exception: May carry 12–13 players for max 2 consecutive weeks and 28 total days.
3. May exceed 15 players if:

   - Hardship exception granted, or
   - Player in Anti-Drug Program in-patient treatment and out ≥3 games.

4. For each Two-Way Player, both min and max roster size increase by 1.
5. Non-Season periods: max 21 players across all lists.
   **Edge Cases**: Days with <14 players count toward 28-day limit.
   **Citation**: Article XXIX, Sec. 2

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Two-Way Player Roster Rules
**Purpose**: Used to define status of Two-Way Players during season and postseason.
**Inputs**:

- Contract type (Two-Way vs converted Standard NBA)
- Date relative to last Regular Season game
  **Rules**:

1. In-season: Two-Way Player must be on Active/Inactive List while with NBA team; otherwise on Two-Way List.
2. Postseason: Two-Way Players ineligible for postseason rosters or games.
3. Exception: if converted to Standard NBA Contract before last Regular Season game, player may join postseason roster.
   **Edge Cases**: Conversion under Article II, Sec. 11(g) or 11(h) is required for eligibility.
   **Citation**: Article XXIX, Sec. 3

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Minimum League-Wide Roster Size Enforcement
**Purpose**: Used to ensure Teams maintain sufficient league-wide roster sizes.
**Inputs**:

- Aggregate average roster size across two consecutive seasons (excluding Two-Way)
- Thresholds (14.25 or 14.5)
  **Rules**:

1. If <14.25 average for 2 straight seasons:

   - Teams must carry exactly 15 players (no 14-player option).
   - 12–13 player exception increases to 13–14.

2. If <14.5 average for 2 straight seasons:

   - Players Association may amend CBA to reduce max Two-Way contracts from 3 → 2.

3. Method: calculate Duty Days for each player, compare to league-wide thresholds.
   **Edge Cases**: Trigger applies only after 2 consecutive low-average seasons.
   **Citation**: Article XXIX, Sec. 5

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Award Eligibility – Games Played Requirement
**Purpose**: Used to determine player eligibility for major awards and honors.
**Inputs**:

- Games played count
- Minutes played per game
- Injury status
- Season dates
  **Rules**:

1. Player eligible if:

   - Played ≥65 games, OR
   - Played ≥62 games, had season-ending injury, and played ≥85% of team's games before injury.

2. Game counts if ≥20 minutes played; up to 2 games may count at 15–19 minutes.
3. Player can challenge ineligibility via:

   - Award Eligibility Grievance (prove team willfully limited minutes/games to block eligibility).
   - Extraordinary Circumstances Challenge (prove extraordinary circumstances prevented meeting threshold).
     **Edge Cases**:

- Award Eligibility Grievance only if eligibility affects Higher Max or Designated Vet status.
- Season-ending injury defined by independent physician determination.
  **Citation**: Article XXIX, Sec. 6
