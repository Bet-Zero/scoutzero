# Article XXVII – Right of Set-Off

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Set-Off Calculation
**Purpose**: Used to reduce a Team’s liability for unearned Base Compensation when a terminated player signs with another team.
**Inputs**:

- First Team liability (unearned Base Compensation, including deferred)
- Player Years of Service at termination
- Compensation earned from Subsequent Team(s)
  **Rules**:

1. Calculate total compensation earned from Subsequent Team(s) each year.
2. Subtract applicable Minimum Salary (0 Years = rookie min; ≥1 Year = 1-year veteran min).
3. If ≤0, no reduction. If >0, reduce liability by 50% of amount.
4. Applies only to pro basketball team compensation (cash & non-cash, excluding benefits, travel, housing).
5. Non-NBA Subsequent Team comp is grossed up by dividing net pay by 0.65 unless player proves otherwise.
6. First Team may require proof of Subsequent Team compensation.
   **Edge Cases**: Team not required to enforce set-off against non-NBA teams.
   **Citation**: Article XXVII, Sec. 1

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Successive Terminations
**Purpose**: Used to determine which Team benefits from set-off rights after multiple terminations.
**Inputs**:

- Sequence of terminated contracts with same player
  **Rules**:

1. First Team to terminate retains set-off rights until liability eliminated.
2. Once satisfied, rights pass in order to subsequent terminating Teams.
   **Edge Cases**: Applies sequentially across multiple contracts.
   **Citation**: Article XXVII, Sec. 2

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Deferred Compensation Set-Off
**Purpose**: Used to calculate set-off amounts when unearned Deferred Compensation exists.
**Inputs**:

- Unearned Deferred Compensation
- Prime rate at time of agreement
  **Rules**:
- Discount deferred amounts annually at prime rate when applying set-off.
  **Edge Cases**: Prime rate changes after agreement don’t affect discount rate.
  **Citation**: Article XXVII, Sec. 3

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Waiver of Set-Off Right
**Purpose**: Used to allow modification or elimination of set-off rights.
**Inputs**:

- Existing Player Contract amendment
  **Rules**:
- Teams and players may modify or eliminate set-off only through amendment allowed under Article II, Sec. 3(p).
  **Edge Cases**: Cannot waive at time of original signing.
  **Citation**: Article XXVII, Sec. 4

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Stretched Protected Salary – Set-Off Application
**Purpose**: Used to determine set-off allocation when protected salary is stretched.
**Inputs**:

- First Contract stretch election (mandatory or elective)
- Subsequent Team earnings by season
- Remaining Salary Cap Years under First Contract
  **Rules**:

1. Set-off calculated based on unearned Base Comp for each original contract year (not stretch schedule).
2. Set-off allocated equally across all stretched payments for that year.
3. NBA may direct Subsequent NBA Team to withhold and remit set-off amounts.
4. Examples:

   - Example X: \$600K set-off spread evenly across 3 stretched seasons.
   - Example Y: Set-off allocated across 5 stretched seasons proportionally.
   - Example Z: Additional Subsequent Team earnings further reduce stretched payments proportionally.
     **Edge Cases**: No set-off for Subsequent Team earnings in seasons after original contract term.
     **Citation**: Article XXVII, Sec. 5
