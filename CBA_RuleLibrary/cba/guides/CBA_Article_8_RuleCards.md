# Article VIII – Rookie Scale

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Rookie Scale Contract Structure
**Purpose**: Used to determine contract terms for First Round Picks.
**Inputs**:

- Draft position
- Contract year (1st–4th)
  **Rules**:

1. Contract covers 2 guaranteed seasons.
2. Team holds option for 3rd season (must be exercised between end of 1st season and Oct 31).
3. Team holds option for 4th season (must be exercised between end of 2nd season and Oct 31).
   **Edge Cases**: Options cannot be exercised outside designated windows.
   **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 1(a)

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Rookie Salary Scale Application
**Purpose**: Used to determine Rookie Scale Amounts for First Round Picks.
**Inputs**:

- Rookie Salary Scale for given year
- Draft selection number
- Signing date (relative to Jan 10)
  **Rules**:

1. Rookie Scale determined by first season covered.
2. Salary amounts tied to draft slot.
3. Unsigned picks after Jan 10 lose salary daily (amount ÷ season days).
   **Edge Cases**: Late signings reduce salary proportionately.
   **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 1(b)(i)

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Rookie Scale Adjustment for Forfeited Picks
**Purpose**: Used to adjust Rookie Salary Scale when first-round picks are forfeited.
**Inputs**:

- Number of forfeited picks
- Draft position affected
  **Rules**:

1. Remove mid-first-round Rookie Scale slot(s) based on number of forfeitures.

   - 1 pick: remove 15th pick slot.
   - 2 picks: remove 15th and 16th.
   - 3 picks: remove 14th, 15th, 16th.
   - > 3 picks: continue procedure outward.

2. Remaining salaries shift accordingly.
   **Edge Cases**: Ensures scale remains consistent for 30 slots.
   **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 1(b)(ii)

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Rookie Scale Salary Limits and Protections
**Purpose**: Used to set salary range and guarantee protections in Rookie Scale Contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Draft slot Rookie Scale Amount
- Player Minimum Salary
  **Rules**:

1. Base Compensation ≥ greater of 80% Rookie Scale or Minimum Salary.
2. Max Salary + Unlikely Bonuses ≤ 120% of Rookie Scale.
3. No signing bonuses/loans (except international buyouts above exclusion).
4. Contracts must guarantee ≥80% of Rookie Scale for 1st–2nd seasons + 3rd-year option.
5. 4th-year terms mirror 3rd year with % increase from Rookie Scale.
   **Edge Cases**: International payments follow Article VII, Sec. 3(e).
   **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 1(c)

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Rookie Trade Bonus Cap
**Purpose**: Used to prevent Rookie Scale Contracts from exceeding allowed max in trades.
**Inputs**:

- Rookie Scale Amount
- Player trade bonus
  **Rules**:
- If trade bonus pushes Salary + Unlikely Bonuses >120% of Rookie Scale, bonus is reduced to fit.
  **Edge Cases**: Applies only to Rookie Scale Contracts.
  **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 1(d)

---

## Rule Card 6

**Name**: Later-Signed First Round Picks
**Purpose**: Used to determine contract rules for unsigned First Round Picks after 3 seasons.
**Inputs**:

- Draft year
- Whether player played NCAA in 3-year window
- Team Cap Room availability
  **Rules**:

1. If unsigned after 3 years (and no NCAA play), player may sign:

   - Rookie Scale Contract, or
   - 3+ year contract (not including option) if team has Cap Room >120% of Rookie Scale slot.
     **Edge Cases**: Cap Room required for alternative contract.
     **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 2

---

## Rule Card 7

**Name**: Loss of Draft Rights
**Purpose**: Used to determine when a team loses rights to a First Round Pick.
**Inputs**:

- Team action (tender, withdrawal, renunciation)
- Signing timeline after draft
  **Rules**:

1. Team loses rights if:

   - No Required Tender made,
   - Tender withdrawn,
   - Rights renounced, or
   - Player not signed within 1 year of Subsequent Draft.

2. Player becomes Rookie Free Agent.
3. Team that lost rights cannot re-sign player until after another team contract ends (played out or waived).
   **Edge Cases**: Applies equally to Subsequent Draft selections.
   **Citation**: Article VIII, Sec. 3
