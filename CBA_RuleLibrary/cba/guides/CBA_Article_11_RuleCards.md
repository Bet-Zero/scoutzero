# Article XI – Free Agency

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Free Agency Negotiation Windows
**Purpose**: Used to determine when Free Agents may negotiate and sign contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Player type (Unrestricted FA, Restricted FA, Non-Draft Rookie)
- Season end date
- Moratorium period dates
  **Rules**:

1. UFAs: may negotiate from June 30 (6 p.m. ET) and sign after Moratorium.
2. RFAs: may negotiate with Prior Team from June 30, sign Offer Sheets from first day of Moratorium, and sign with Prior Team after Moratorium.
3. Players finishing season on roster: may negotiate with that Team immediately after season ends.
4. Non-Draft Rookies: may negotiate after conclusion of first eligible Draft.
   **Edge Cases**: Options/ETOs determine FA status.
   **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 1(a)

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Qualifying Offer – General Requirements
**Purpose**: Used to determine structure of Qualifying Offers that create Restricted Free Agency.
**Inputs**:

- Player contract type (Rookie Scale, Two-Way, Veteran)
- Player’s draft position and service years
- Player’s 4th-year salary or prior contract salary
  **Rules**:

1. Must be for 1 year, 100% protected for skill/injury.
2. Salary and bonuses follow detailed formulas by player type:

   - Rookie Scale FAs: QO = 4th-year salary + % increase, with Starter Criteria adjustments.
   - Two-Way FAs: QO varies between Standard NBA and Two-Way depending on eligibility.
   - Other RFAs: QO = 135% (or 125% if pre-2023 contract) of prior salary, subject to Minimum-Plus test.
     **Edge Cases**: Starter Criteria can shift QO to higher draft slot equivalents (9th or 21st pick).
     **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 1(e)

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: No Individually-Negotiated Right of First Refusal
**Purpose**: Used to prevent custom ROFR clauses in contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Player Contract terms
  **Rules**:

1. No Player Contract may include a unique ROFR.
2. ROFR applies only as set forth for RFAs.
3. ROFR cannot be triggered by non-NBA contracts.
   **Edge Cases**: Applies to all contracts regardless of negotiation.
   **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 2

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Withholding Services and Free Agency Eligibility
**Purpose**: Used to determine whether a player qualifies as a Veteran Free Agent.
**Inputs**:

- Player service time rendered
- Days withheld after contract start
  **Rules**:

1. If a player withholds services >30 days in final contract year, he is not deemed to have completed his contract.
2. Such a player cannot become a Veteran FA or sign with another team unless Prior Team agrees.
   **Edge Cases**: Restriction applies across professional leagues.
   **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 3

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Timing of Qualifying Offers and Maximum Qualifying Offers
**Purpose**: Used to establish deadlines and mechanics for issuing QOs and MQOs.
**Inputs**:

- Player type (Rookie Scale, Veteran, Two-Way)
- Team’s decision to tender
  **Rules**:

1. Rookie Scale: QO may be issued after 2nd option year until June 29; if issued, player becomes RFA on July 1.
2. Teams may also offer a 5-year Maximum Qualifying Offer (max salary, 8% raises, no options/ETOs, full protection).
3. Veterans/Two-Way: QO may be issued until June 29 to create RFA status.
   **Edge Cases**: If no QO made, player becomes UFA.
   **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 4

---

## Rule Card 6

**Name**: Acceptance, Withdrawal, and Expiration of Qualifying Offers
**Purpose**: Used to define how QOs remain valid or expire.
**Inputs**:

- QO issuance date
- Player acceptance timeline
  **Rules**:

1. Player has until Oct 1 to accept QO.
2. Team may withdraw by July 13 without consent; later withdrawals require player consent.
3. If withdrawn, player becomes UFA (and deemed renounced if after July 14).
4. Acceptance deadline may be extended, but not past March 1.
   **Edge Cases**: Medical disability disclosure bars acceptance unless Team consents.
   **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 4(c)

---

## Rule Card 7

**Name**: Restricted Free Agency – ROFR Mechanics
**Purpose**: Used to regulate Offer Sheets and Right of First Refusal.
**Inputs**:

- Offer Sheet details (Principal Terms, salary structure, term)
- ROFR Team’s salary/exception availability
  **Rules**:

1. RFA may sign Offer Sheet >1 season (or >2 if QO + MQO issued).
2. New Team must have and maintain Room for contract.
3. ROFR Team may match using Room or Exceptions.
4. Matching binds player to ROFR Team on Principal Terms for 1 year (no amendments).
5. Deadlines: ROFR Team must match within 1–2 days (or July 7 if during Moratorium).
   **Edge Cases**:

- 1–2 Year Service Offer Sheets capped at Non-Taxpayer MLE in years 1–2 (“Arenas Provision”).
- ROFR Team restrictions on trading matched players for 1 year.
  **Citation**: Article XI, Sec. 5
