# Article II – Player Contracts

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Contract Amendments – General Limitation
**Purpose**: Used to determine when Teams/players may alter a Uniform Player Contract.
**Inputs**:

- Type of contract (Standard, Two-Way, Exhibit 10)
- Proposed amendment (e.g., bonuses, trade rights)
  **Rules**:

1. No amendments to standard contracts except as explicitly allowed in Articles II, VII, XII.
2. No amendments to Two-Way or Exhibit 10 contracts except as specified.
3. Option Years and Early Termination Options cannot be added to Two-Way or Exhibit 10 contracts.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Limited carve-outs exist in Sections 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, and 12.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 2

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Allowable Contract Amendments – Compensation & Bonuses
**Purpose**: Used to determine what changes Teams/players may negotiate in a contract.
**Inputs**:

- Contract type (Standard, Exhibit 1A, Exhibit 1B)
- Compensation structure
- Bonus type (signing, performance, condition)
  **Rules**:

1. Teams may set Compensation, lump-sum bonuses, and payment schedules within defined limits.
2. Bonuses must be tied to measurable benchmarks (official NBA stats, league honors).
3. Offseason participation/education bonuses allowed if compliant.
4. Compensation may be paid in 12 or 36 installments starting Nov 1; advance payments capped at 80% of protected amount or 50% of base salary.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Players at Minimum Salary have narrower amendment rights.
- Bonuses cannot be based on improvement over prior performance.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 3(a–d)

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Trade and Consent Amendments
**Purpose**: Used to determine when trade rights can be waived or restricted.
**Inputs**:

- Player contract clauses (Exhibit 1/1A, 3, 4)
- Trade restriction terms
  **Rules**:

1. Teams may restrict their right to trade a player.
2. A player may waive his “no-trade consent” rights.
3. Players may receive Compensation for being traded (Exhibit 4).
4. Contracts may include sign-and-trade preconditions (Exhibit 8).
   **Edge Cases**:

- Training Camp Contracts (Exhibit 9) limited to 6 per team; must be minimum salary, 1-year, non-guaranteed.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 3(g–r)

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Compensation Protection – Categories
**Purpose**: Used to determine when guaranteed money survives termination.
**Inputs**:

- Reason for termination (skill, death, injury, illness, mental disability)
- Contract Exhibit 2 provisions
  **Rules**:

1. If protected, Base Compensation remains payable even if terminated.
2. Standard carve-outs: no protection if caused by misconduct, banned substances, crimes, or breach.
3. Death protection capped at \$30M aggregate.
4. Future-year protection percentages cannot exceed prior-year unless conditional.
5. Payment of owed protected salary depends on remaining balance (≤\$500K vs >\$500K triggers extended payout schedules).
   **Edge Cases**:

- Optional conditions may tie protection to team deadlines, benchmarks, insurance.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 4

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Minimum Player Salary Rule
**Purpose**: Used to enforce salary floors in all contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Contract type (Standard, 10-Day, Rest-of-Season, Two-Way)
- Salary Cap year
  **Rules**:

1. Every player must receive at least the Minimum Annual Salary Scale for that Cap Year.
2. Contracts below minimum automatically amended July 1 to meet new scale.
3. Minimum-salary contracts may advance up to 7.5% of base if protected.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Ten-Day, Rest-of-Season, Two-Way have their own applications.
- Exhibit 1A language must confirm “no bonuses of any kind.”
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 6

---

## Rule Card 6

**Name**: Maximum Annual Salary
**Purpose**: Used to cap first-year salary and renegotiations/extensions.
**Inputs**:

- Years of Service (<7, 7–9, 10+)
- Salary Cap value
- Higher Max Criteria (All-NBA, DPOY, MVP)
- Prior contract salary
  **Rules**:

1. <7 Years Service: max 25% of Cap (30% if Higher Max met in rookie extension).
2. 7–9 Years Service: max 30% of Cap (35% if Designated Vet & Higher Max met).
3. 10+ Years Service: max 35% of Cap.
4. Renegotiations/extensions follow same structure.
5. Trade bonuses cannot push salary over these limits.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Rookie extensions can specify “if meet Higher Max in year 4, raise to 30%.”
- Designated Vet contracts/extensions capped at 35%.
- Contracts above 25% for 5th Year Eligible Players must last 4+ seasons.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 7

---

## Rule Card 7

**Name**: 10-Day Contracts
**Purpose**: Used to allow temporary signings during the season.
**Inputs**:

- Date (must be Jan 5 or later)
- Team roster size
- Number of prior 10-Day contracts with same player
  **Rules**:

1. Term = longer of 10 days or 3 games.
2. Salary = prorated Minimum Salary.
3. Max 2 contracts per player per team per season.
4. Roster limits: 0 allowed at 12 players; 1 at 13; 2 at 14; 3 at 15.
5. No contracts extending past team’s final regular-season game.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Hardship rule overrides restrictions.
- Terminated by written notice, not waivers.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 9

---

## Rule Card 8

**Name**: Rest-of-Season Contracts
**Purpose**: Used to sign players after season starts for remainder of year.
**Inputs**:

- Season timing (after 1st day)
  **Rules**:
- Must be at least Minimum Salary.
- Ends at season’s conclusion.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Two-Way contracts excluded.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 10

---

## Rule Card 9

**Name**: Two-Way Contracts
**Purpose**: Used to sign developmental players splitting time between NBA and G-League.
**Inputs**:

- Player’s Years of Service (<4 except carve-out case)
- Contract length (≤2 years, no options/ETO)
- Team roster (max 3 Two-Way players)
- Salary Cap year
  **Rules**:

1. Salary = 50% of rookie min, prorated by days.
2. No bonuses, incentives, deferred comp, or loans.
3. Max 50 NBA games per season; prorated if signed late.
4. Max 90 “under-15 games” across team per year.
5. Term ≤ 2 seasons.
6. Max 3 Two-Way seasons with same team.
7. Team holds exclusive rights; conversion option to Standard NBA Contract required.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Conversion from Exhibit 10 available before season start.
- Max protection = \$75K (2023–24 baseline, scaled with Cap).
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 11

---

## Rule Card 10

**Name**: Bonuses – Restrictions
**Purpose**: Used to regulate incentive and signing bonus structures.
**Inputs**:

- Bonus type (signing, incentive, Exhibit 10)
- Contract type
  **Rules**:

1. Incentive Comp ≤ 20% of base salary.
2. Signing bonus ≤ 15% of comp in standard contracts; ≤10% in offer sheets.
3. Offseason program bonuses capped at 2 weeks; can be waived for national team or medical exceptions.
4. Roster bonuses (e.g., “on roster on X date”) prohibited.
5. Incentive conditions cannot be amended mid-contract.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Exhibit 10 bonuses capped at \$75K (2023–24 baseline).
- Exhibit 10 + Two-Way conversions subject to conversion protection rules.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 12

---

## Rule Card 11

**Name**: General Contract Rules
**Purpose**: Used to enforce universal baseline contract conditions.
**Inputs**:

- Training camp participation
- Salary payment structure
- Termination rules
  **Rules**:

1. Players cannot attend camp/practice without an active contract.
2. Contracts must pay ≥10% of annual salary as Current Base Comp in scheduled installments.
3. “Lack of skill” includes termination for financial planning reasons.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Post-termination obligations may still exist if protections apply.
  **Citation**: Article II, Sec. 13
