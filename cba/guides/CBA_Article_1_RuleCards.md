# Article I – Definitions

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Average Player Salary
**Purpose**: Used to determine average salary benchmarks (affects exceptions, max/min contracts).
**Inputs**:

- Total Salaries leaguewide
- Number of Teams (excluding expansion teams in first 2 years)
  **Rules**:

1. Add up all Total Salaries for a Salary Cap Year.
2. Include additional amounts paid under Article VII, Sec. 2(c).
3. Divide by (number of Teams × 13.2).
   **Edge Cases**:

- Expansion Teams excluded for first 2 Salary Cap Years.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(f)

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Basketball Related Income (BRI)
**Purpose**: Used to calculate league revenues, Salary Cap, and related financial metrics.
**Inputs**:

- All basketball-related revenue sources (per Article VII, Sec. 1(a), (b))
  **Rules**:
- Defined fully in Article VII; BRI drives Salary Cap and tax thresholds.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Specific inclusions/exclusions listed in Article VII.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(h)
  **See Also**: Article VII

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Base Compensation
**Purpose**: Used to calculate a player’s guaranteed pay excluding bonuses.
**Inputs**:

- Player Compensation package
  **Rules**:
- Base Compensation = total Compensation – all bonuses.
  **Edge Cases**: None specified.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(g)

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Compensation
**Purpose**: Used to determine a player’s total contractual earnings.
**Inputs**:

- Player Contract terms
- Payments (direct or designated)
  **Rules**:
- Includes all amounts earned or payable under a Player Contract.
- Applies even if contract terminated.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Includes payments to third parties designated by player.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(k)

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Deferred Compensation
**Purpose**: Used to determine when salary counts toward Team Salary and cash flow.
**Inputs**:

- Player Contract payment schedule
- Season end date (May 1 cutoff)
  **Rules**:

1. Deferred Compensation = amounts payable after May 1 following a Season.
2. Determined strictly by timing in the contract.
3. Funding/escrow status does not affect classification.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Split between Deferred Base Compensation vs bonuses.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(o)

---

## Rule Card 6

**Name**: Designated Veteran Player Contract
**Purpose**: Used to allow a team to sign its veteran star to an extended max-level deal.
**Inputs**:

- Player is a Designated Veteran Player
- Qualifying Veteran Free Agent status
- Salary Cap value
  **Rules**:

1. Contract covers 5 Seasons.
2. Year 1 Salary = 30–35% of Salary Cap (exact % negotiated).
3. Salary increases/decreases follow Article VII, Sec. 5(a)(2).
   **Edge Cases**:

- Only available under specific eligibility rules (see Article II, Sec. 7).
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(q)
  **See Also**: Article II, Sec. 7; Article VII

---

## Rule Card 7

**Name**: Designated Veteran Player Extension
**Purpose**: Used to extend a qualifying veteran’s deal beyond the standard max rules.
**Inputs**:

- Player = Designated Veteran Player
- Current contract terms
- Salary Cap value
  **Rules**:

1. Extension covers 6 Seasons from signature.
2. Year 1 Salary of extension = 30–35% of Salary Cap in first extended year.
3. Annual increases follow Article VII, Sec. 5(a)(3).
   **Edge Cases**: Must meet eligibility and timing requirements.
   **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(r)

---

## Rule Card 8

**Name**: Early Termination Option (ETO)
**Purpose**: Used when a player can shorten his contract term.
**Inputs**:

- Contract terms specifying ETO
  **Rules**:

1. Grants player right to end contract early.
2. Effective Season = first Season after the ETO can be exercised.
   **Edge Cases**: None beyond Article XII rules.
   **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(u)–(v)

---

## Rule Card 9

**Name**: Exception
**Purpose**: Used to determine when a team may exceed the Salary Cap.
**Inputs**:

- Type of Exception invoked (MLE, Bi-Annual, TPE, etc.)
  **Rules**:
- Exceptions permit Team Salary > Salary Cap in specific cases.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Exceptions expire or have usage restrictions (see Article VII).
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(x)

---

## Rule Card 10

**Name**: Free Agent
**Purpose**: Used to determine which players can be signed without trade or draft rights.
**Inputs**:

- Player’s contract status
  **Rules**:
- Free Agent = (i) Veteran FA, (ii) Rookie FA, (iii) player waived, (iv) expired 10-Day contract.
  **Edge Cases**:
- 10-Day FA must have either completed or been released early.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(bb)

---

## Rule Card 11

**Name**: Maximum Annual Salary
**Purpose**: Used to cap how much a player can earn in the first year of a deal.
**Inputs**:

- Years of service
- Salary Cap for that year
  **Rules**:
- Max Annual Salary calculated under Article II, Sec. 7.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Supermax/Designated rules override standard.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(gg)

---

## Rule Card 12

**Name**: Minimum Annual Salary & Scale
**Purpose**: Used to enforce floor salary by years of service.
**Inputs**:

- Salary Cap year
- Player years of service
  **Rules**:

1. League publishes a Minimum Salary Scale table each year.
2. Table is adjusted annually based on Salary Cap increases.
3. Contracts must meet or exceed this amount.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Special prorations for 10-Day or partial-season deals.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(ii)–(kk)

---

## Rule Card 13

**Name**: Moratorium Period
**Purpose**: Used to restrict transactions at start of league year.
**Inputs**:

- Salary Cap Year calendar
  **Rules**:
- Runs July 1, 12:01 a.m. → July 6, 12:00 p.m. ET each year.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Applies even if July 6 is non-business day.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(mm)

---

## Rule Card 14

**Name**: Qualifying Offer
**Purpose**: Used to extend restricted rights to a player.
**Inputs**:

- Prior Team
- Rookie Scale / contract terms
  **Rules**:
- Defined in Article XI, Sec. 1(e). Tendering QO makes player a Restricted Free Agent.
  **Edge Cases**: Acceptance deadlines and salary rules apply.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(xx)

---

## Rule Card 15

**Name**: Rookie Salary Scale & Contract
**Purpose**: Used to determine required salary amounts for First Round Picks.
**Inputs**:

- Draft position
- Salary Cap year
  **Rules**:

1. Rookie Scale = published annually after Cap set.
2. Scale adjusts each year by Cap growth %.
3. Rookie Scale Contract = mandatory for First Round Picks, with fixed ranges (80–120%).
   **Edge Cases**:

- 4th year options and QO increases follow fixed % columns.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(hhh)–(jjj)

---

## Rule Card 16

**Name**: Salary Cap
**Purpose**: Used to limit total Team Salary.
**Inputs**:

- BRI calculations
- CBA adjustment formulas
  **Rules**:
- Cap set each year (July 1–June 30).
- Teams cannot exceed except via Exceptions.
  **Edge Cases**: Adjusted annually; Expansion teams special cases.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(mmm)–(nnn)

---

## Rule Card 17

**Name**: Team Salary
**Purpose**: Used to measure a Team’s payroll compliance with the Cap and Tax.
**Inputs**:

- All player Salaries (active and former)
- Additions/reductions per Article VII
  **Rules**:
- Team Salary = sum of all salaries + adjustments – credits.
  **Edge Cases**:
- Includes deferred pay, waived players, Exhibit 10, Two-Way, etc.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(uuu)

---

## Rule Card 18

**Name**: Traded Player
**Purpose**: Used to determine trade eligibility and matching rules.
**Inputs**:

- Player Contract assignment
  **Rules**:
- A player becomes a Traded Player when his contract is assigned to another Team (not waivers).
  **Edge Cases**: Waiver claims not considered trades.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(xxx)

---

## Rule Card 19

**Name**: Years of Service
**Purpose**: Used to determine contract rights, minimums, max eligibility.
**Inputs**:

- Active or Inactive List status
- Season participation
  **Rules**:

1. Credited if player on Active/Inactive List at least 1 day in season.
2. Not credited if player withholds services >30 days after start.
3. Not credited if RFA tender not signed by Mar 1.
4. Max 1 year credit per season.
   **Edge Cases**:

- Disapproved contracts do not earn credit.
  **Citation**: Article I, Sec. 1(iiii)
