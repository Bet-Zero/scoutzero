# Exhibit B & C – Rookie Scale and Minimum Salary Scale

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Baseline Rookie Salary Scale
**Purpose**: Used to determine Rookie Scale contract salaries for First Round Picks.
**Inputs**:

- Draft pick number (1–30)
- Contract year (1st–4th)
- Rookie Salary Scale table for given season
  **Rules**:

1. First and second-year salaries fixed by draft position per Exhibit B.
2. 3rd-year salary = fixed increase over 2nd-year salary.
3. 4th-year option = percentage increase over 3rd-year salary per draft slot.
4. Qualifying Offer after 4th year = percentage increase over 4th-year salary per draft slot.
   **Edge Cases**: Rookie Scale contracts must remain within 80–120% of slot amount (see Article VIII).
   **Citation**: Exhibit B

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Baseline Minimum Annual Salary Scale
**Purpose**: Used to determine the minimum allowable salary based on Years of Service.
**Inputs**:

- Years of Service (0–10+)
- Contract year (1–5)
- Minimum Salary Scale table for given season
  **Rules**:

1. Players must receive at least the salary listed for their Years of Service and contract year.
2. Table provides rising amounts for each additional season of service.
3. 10+ years of service uses final row of Exhibit C.
   **Edge Cases**: Amounts subject to proration for 10-Day or Rest-of-Season contracts.
   **Citation**: Exhibit C
