# Article XXIV – Prohibition of No-Trade Contracts

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: General Limitation on No-Trade Clauses
**Purpose**: Used to establish the default rule that contracts cannot block trades.
**Inputs**:

- Player Contract terms
  **Rules**:

1. No Player Contract may restrict a Team’s right to assign (trade) the contract.
   **Edge Cases**: Exceptions exist under Section 2.
   **Citation**: Article XXIV, Sec. 1

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Trade Bonus Rules
**Purpose**: Used to regulate compensation a player can receive if traded.
**Inputs**:

- Contract Exhibit 4 terms
- Remaining Base Compensation
- Whether Option Year exercised
  **Rules**:

1. Trade bonus payable only the first time a contract is traded.

   - Exception: If contract signed as part of trade, bonus applies only on second trade.

2. Bonus ≤15% of remaining Base Compensation (excluding unexercised Option Year).
3. Trade bonus can only be paid once.
   **Edge Cases**: Initial trade bonuses void if tied to prearranged trade.
   **Citation**: Article XXIV, Sec. 2(a)

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Limited No-Trade Rights for Veterans
**Purpose**: Used to determine when veterans may negotiate no-trade protection.
**Inputs**:

- Player Years of Service (≥8)
- Years of Service with current Team (≥4)
  **Rules**:
- Contracts may include no-trade clauses only if player meets both thresholds.
  **Edge Cases**: Applies only to current Team at time of signing.
  **Citation**: Article XXIV, Sec. 2(b)
