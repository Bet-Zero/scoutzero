# Article IX – Length of Player Contracts

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Maximum Contract Term
**Purpose**: Used to determine maximum length of Player Contracts and Extensions.
**Inputs**:

- Player type (Rookie, Veteran, Designated Veteran, Qualifying Veteran FA)
- Contract type (New, Extension, Rookie Extension, Veteran Extension)
  **Rules**:

1. Standard contracts may cover up to 4 seasons.
2. Qualifying Veteran Free Agent re-signing with Prior Team: up to 5 seasons.
3. Rookie Scale Extension: up to 6 seasons.
4. Veteran Extension (non-Designated): up to 5 seasons.
5. Designated Veteran Player Extension: must be 6 seasons.
6. Maximum terms include any Option Years.
   **Edge Cases**: Specific shorter terms may be provided elsewhere in the CBA.
   **Citation**: Article IX, Sec. 1

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Computation of Contract Seasons
**Purpose**: Used to determine how to count Seasons in contracts signed mid-year.
**Inputs**:

- Signing date (relative to NBA season calendar)
- Extension vs new contract
  **Rules**:

1. If contract/extension signed after a season starts, that season counts as one full season of term.
2. If extension signed between end of season and June 30, the just-completed season counts as one season of term.
   **Edge Cases**: None beyond timing windows.
   **Citation**: Article IX, Sec. 2
