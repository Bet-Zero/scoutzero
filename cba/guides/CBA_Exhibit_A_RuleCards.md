# Exhibit A – Uniform Player Contract

## Rule Card 1

**Name**: Assignment Rights
**Purpose**: Used to enforce Team rights to trade/assign a Player Contract.
**Inputs**:

- Active Player Contract
  **Rules**:

1. Team may assign contract to another NBA Team at any time.
2. Player must accept assignment and continue contract under new Team.
   **Edge Cases**: None beyond waiver and no-trade protections elsewhere in CBA.
   **Citation**: Exhibit A, Sec. 10(a)

---

## Rule Card 2

**Name**: Termination for Lack of Skill or Ability
**Purpose**: Used to determine Player compensation rights if terminated for insufficient skill.
**Inputs**:

- Termination date (before or after Jan 10)
- Injury status at termination
  **Rules**:

1. If terminated before Jan 10 while injured, Player continues to receive Base Compensation until fit to play, capped at season end.
2. If terminated after Jan 10, Player receives full Base Compensation for season.
3. Player terminated for lack of skill otherwise only entitled to earned pay.
   **Edge Cases**: Protections subject to Exhibit 3 (Prior Injury Exclusion).
   **Citation**: Exhibit A, Sec. 16(a)(iii)

---

## Rule Card 3

**Name**: Termination for Breach of Contract
**Purpose**: Used to govern when Team may terminate for refusal or material breach.
**Inputs**:

- Player actions (refusal, neglect, breach of duties)
  **Rules**:
- Team may terminate contract immediately upon material breach.
  **Edge Cases**: None specified.
  **Citation**: Exhibit A, Sec. 16(a)(iv)

---

## Rule Card 4

**Name**: Termination for Injury or Disability
**Purpose**: Used to determine Player compensation rights after injury.
**Inputs**:

- Timing of injury (current vs prior season)
- Fitness to play
  **Rules**:

1. If injured while playing for Team: Player entitled to full Base Compensation for season (minus insurance/worker’s comp).
2. If injured in prior season/offseason before camp: Player entitled only to training expenses, housing, travel, per diem.
3. If terminated during training camp: same limited entitlements as above.
   **Edge Cases**: Exhibit 3 may reduce protections if Prior Injury Exclusion attached.
   **Citation**: Exhibit A, Sec. 16(b–d)

---

## Rule Card 5

**Name**: Termination After Season Start
**Purpose**: Used to calculate owed salary when Player waived mid-season.
**Inputs**:

- Termination date relative to Regular Season
- Days elapsed in season
  **Rules**:
- Player entitled to prorated portion of annual salary (days played ÷ total season days) plus travel expenses home.
  **Edge Cases**: Does not apply if protected by other provisions.
  **Citation**: Exhibit A, Sec. 16(e)

---

## Rule Card 6

**Name**: Waiver Procedure
**Purpose**: Used to regulate mechanics of releasing Players via waivers.
**Inputs**:

- Team request for waivers
- Waiver claim status
- NBA Constitution and By-Laws rules
  **Rules**:

1. Team must request waivers from Commissioner before termination.
2. Request cannot be withdrawn.
3. Other Teams may claim within 48 hours; priority by lowest standing.
4. If claimed, Player contract assigned to claiming Team.
5. If unclaimed, contract terminates after waiver period.
6. NBA must notify NBPA of disposition.
   **Edge Cases**: Ties in waiver claims resolved by H2H record, then coin toss.
   **Citation**: Exhibit A, Sec. 16(f); NBA By-Laws Sec. 5

---

## Rule Card 7

**Name**: Waiver Price
**Purpose**: Used to define standard cost of claiming Player on waivers.
**Inputs**:

- Waiver claim
  **Rules**:
- Waiver price set at \$1,000 per Player.
  **Edge Cases**: None.
  **Citation**: NBA By-Laws Sec. 5.02

---

## Rule Card 8

**Name**: 30-Day Restriction on Waived Players
**Purpose**: Used to restrict trading Players acquired via waivers.
**Inputs**:

- Waiver acquisition date
  **Rules**:
- Team acquiring Player on waivers cannot trade him for 30 days (or until start of next season if acquired offseason).
  **Edge Cases**: Offseason acquisitions count from start of next season.
  **Citation**: NBA By-Laws Sec. 5.06

---

## Rule Card 9

**Name**: Compensation Protection Exhibit 2
**Purpose**: Used to govern protected salary payments after termination.
**Inputs**:

- Amount owed at separation
- Whether ≤\$500K or >\$500K
- Contract years remaining
  **Rules**:

1. ≤\$500K: pay in regular semi-monthly installments until satisfied.
2. > \$500K:

   - Current season balance paid on normal schedule.
   - Remaining salary aggregated and stretched over (2 × years left) + 1 seasons in equal installments.
     **Edge Cases**: Automatic stretch triggered by termination.
     **Citation**: Exhibit 2

---

## Rule Card 10

**Name**: Prior Injury Exclusion
**Purpose**: Used to limit Team liability for reinjury of known conditions.
**Inputs**:

- Exhibit 3 description of prior injury/condition
  **Rules**:
- Contract may reduce or eliminate compensation if Player reinjures or aggravates listed condition.
  **Edge Cases**: Applies only to specific condition listed in Exhibit 3.
  **Citation**: Exhibit 3

---

## Rule Card 11

**Name**: Trade Payment Exhibit 4
**Purpose**: Used to govern bonuses paid to Players upon trade.
**Inputs**:

- Exhibit 4 terms
- Trade date
  **Rules**:
- Assignor Team must pay Player within 30 days of trade the amount listed in Exhibit 4.
  **Edge Cases**: Subject to max % trade bonus limits (see Article XXIV).
  **Citation**: Exhibit 4

---

## Rule Card 12

**Name**: Exhibit 6 – Physical Exam Condition
**Purpose**: Used to ensure contracts are valid only if Player passes physical.
**Inputs**:

- Date of physical
- Team physician judgment
  **Rules**:

1. Contract invalid unless Player passes physical within 3 business days of signing.
2. Team must report results within 6 business days.
3. Player must provide truthful information.
   **Edge Cases**: Team discretion must be exercised in good faith.
   **Citation**: Exhibit 6

---

## Rule Card 13

**Name**: Exhibit 7 – Conditioning Requirement
**Purpose**: Used to enforce Player’s duty to remain in game-ready condition.
**Inputs**:

- Team physician judgment
- Review by independent physician (if challenged)
  **Rules**:

1. Team may suspend Player week-to-week if judged unfit.
2. Player may appeal to independent physician, whose ruling is binding.
3. During suspension, salary reduced pro rata.
   **Edge Cases**: Suspension not allowed solely due to injury/illness.
   **Citation**: Exhibit 7

---

## Rule Card 14

**Name**: Exhibit 8 – Sign and Trade Condition
**Purpose**: Used to validate sign-and-trade contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Trade execution within 48 hours
- All trade conditions satisfied
  **Rules**:
- Contract void unless traded to specified assignee within 48 hours and conditions met.
  **Edge Cases**: Contract becomes invalid if trade not completed.
  **Citation**: Exhibit 8

---

## Rule Card 15

**Name**: Exhibit 9 – Training Camp Contracts
**Purpose**: Used to govern non-guaranteed one-season camp contracts.
**Inputs**:

- Termination date relative to season start
- Injury status
  **Rules**:

1. If terminated before regular season, Player forfeits compensation (except certain per diem/travel).
2. If injured while playing for Team, Player entitled to \$15,000.
   **Edge Cases**: May include Exhibit 10 terms if attached.
   **Citation**: Exhibit 9

---

## Rule Card 16

**Name**: Exhibit 10 – NBAGL Bonus and Two-Way Conversion
**Purpose**: Used to regulate Exhibit 10 bonuses, G-League assignment, and Two-Way conversion.
**Inputs**:

- Bonus Amount
- Conversion Protection Amount
- Contract type (Exhibit 1A minimum vs Two-Way)
  **Rules**:

1. If waived before season and Player signs/serves with NBAGL affiliate, Team must pay Exhibit 10 bonus.
2. If not waived before season, contract protected for skill/injury up to Conversion Protection Amount.
3. Team may convert Exhibit 10 → Two-Way before season start.
4. If converted, Exhibit 1A void; Player compensation = Two-Way Salary; contract protected at Conversion Protection Amount.
5. Team may later convert Two-Way → Standard NBA Contract with notice; compensation resets to Minimum Salary.
   **Edge Cases**: Bonus rescinded if Player signs outside NBA/NBAGL before 60-day service period.
   **Citation**: Exhibit 10
