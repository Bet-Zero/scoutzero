## November 11: the live path, built switched off (V1 item D2, part 1)

The owner chose to prepare going live now and confirm before it happens.
This code changes nothing in production on its own.

- Server: a SIMULATION package comes from a private local file (demo
  emulator only, as before) or from a private Cloud Storage object
  (`SCOUTZERO_SIMULATION_FINANCIAL_OBJECT` / `..._DRAFT_OBJECT`, as
  `gs://bucket/path`). The object is read only when
  `SCOUTZERO_SIMULATION_LIVE=true`; otherwise it is ignored. Either way the
  bytes must match the pinned `..._SHA256`.
- Client: SIMULATION draft worlds may load, review and Apply outside the
  emulator only when the build sets `VITE_SCOUTZERO_SIMULATION_LIVE=true`.
  Software draft fixtures stay emulator-only.

To go live (needs the owner's confirmation first):

1. Choose where the private packages live. Recommended: a dedicated private
   Cloud Storage bucket in the Firebase project with no public or browser
   access, readable only by the functions' service account. The packages
   never go to GitHub.
2. Upload the current financial and draft packages, and record their
   SHA-256 digests.
3. Set the function settings: the two object names, the two digests and
   `SCOUTZERO_SIMULATION_LIVE=true`.
4. Build the app with `VITE_SCOUTZERO_SIMULATION_LIVE=true`, then deploy
   functions, rules and hosting.
5. Check: create a world, see the SIMULATION label, make one first-round
   trade, reload, and confirm an existing older world still opens (D3).

Rolling back: unset `SCOUTZERO_SIMULATION_LIVE` and rebuild without the
client switch. Worlds created while live keep their data, but their draft
picks stay closed until the switch is turned back on.

## November 11: player trades in SIMULATION worlds (B1 prerequisite)

Before B1, a SIMULATION world could only trade picks for picks. Three
problems stopped every player trade; this change fixes them.

- Tax Salary (A4 gap). Every team's Tax Salary book was needs-input, and the
  Trade Machine then said "Add at least two teams". The private package now
  carries a complete C08 ledger. C08.1 baseline: Team Salary at the start of
  the last 2025-26 Regular Season game (April 12, 2026). The retained source
  has no end-of-season payroll and records no contract signed after April 12,
  so the June 5 Team Salary is used (labelled assumption). C08.7 adds the same
  zero/one-YOS uplift amounts as C07.3. Bonus earned status is unknown, so no
  C08.3/C08.4 change; no post-baseline contract event, trade-bonus
  allocation, grievance or suspension is known. Team Salary includes the
  unrenounced Free Agent Amounts in the source (for example long-retired
  players' holds), so Tax Salary does too, as C08.1 reads. A missing book now
  says so instead of "Add at least two teams".
- Draft fence. In a fenced world every team, entitlement and event commit
  must advance `draftInventoryRevision` by one, but only draft trades did, so
  the rules refused every other save. Ordinary saves now advance it too
  (which also invalidates any pending draft review). Transactional saves read
  the revision inside the transaction; batched saves read it just before the
  batch, and the rules deny the whole batch if another commit came between.
- Moved-player records. An ordinary trade writes the moved player's record
  beside the team document. The draft loader refused any such record; it now
  accepts one only when it names the same team and every one of its fields
  equals the team document's record. Any other field or value still refuses.

Proof: `tests/architect/governedDraftWorld.emulator.test.ts` checks the fence
(batched and transactional saves advance it by one, stale or skipped values are
denied, unfenced worlds stay unfenced) and the record rule (a matching record
loads; a different team, contract, trade bonus, future contract, rights field or
name refuses). `tests/e2e/architect-simulation-player-trade.spec.ts` swaps Tre Jones
for Tre Mann (CHI and CHA, equal $8,000,000 guaranteed salaries), then swaps
the two 2028 firsts in the same world.

What a June 5 world correctly still blocks: expiring contracts after the trade
deadline (CBA2-L03.14), a post-season salary whose next-season protection is
unknown (CBA2-A03.3 needs it), Standard TPE matches beyond 100% + $250,000
(Expanded TPE is B3), and bonus or trade-kicker contracts (post-V1).
Still to do in B1: players and firsts in one trade, and minimum-contract
players (their uplift transfer).

## November 11: players and firsts in one trade (B1 prerequisite)

A governed first-round trade in a SIMULATION world may now include players.

- The pick side keeps the picks-only terms exactly: each first is an existing
  future unconditional first held by the sender, with no new terms.
- Each player must be an unchanged governed Contract on the sender's roster,
  with its exact governed salary basis and an explicit supported matching path
  (Standard TPE, aggregated Standard TPE or Room) carrying its exact salary.
  Cash with players stays blocked (B2), as do Two-Way Contracts (no assignment
  history in these worlds) and sign-and-trades (B6).
- Every team must give and receive something. Only teams sending firsts get a
  draft review; their consideration lists the incoming players' Contracts.
  Salary matching, aprons and roster limits stay with the ordinary validators.
- Apply checks that the computed player moves are exactly the reviewed ones,
  and the review also fences the destination player records. The custody
  receipt records firsts only; players are recorded by the trade event.
- The Validate button now submits the same payload as Apply: no page cap
  projections, and player routes by team code.

Proof: `tests/architect/governedDraftWorld.test.ts` (admission, player-only
sender, gift, missing election, cash and missing salary basis) and the second
test in `tests/e2e/architect-simulation-player-trade.spec.ts` (CHI sends Tre
Jones and its 2028 first for Tre Mann, then the two 2030 firsts swap).

## November 11: minimum-contract players in trades (B1 prerequisite 3)

A zero/one-YOS Minimum uplift (CBA2-C07.3) is an Apron Team Salary row tied to
one Contract, so a trade now moves it with the player
(`src/features/architect/utils/tradeMachine/utils/tradeMinimumUplift.ts`).

- Salary matching is unchanged: the uplift is not Salary. The governed salary
  basis no longer refuses these Contracts.
- Projected Apron Team Salary (and so the elected post-assignment amount, the
  apron levels and hard caps) subtracts the sender's uplift and adds it to the
  receiver, read from the sender's pre-trade books on the world date.
- On commit the sender's row ends (or is removed when it began that day) and
  the receiver gains `apron-team-salary:<team>:minimum-uplift:trade:<id>:<player>`
  from the start of the world day. A sender left without any C07.3 row gets a
  zero row so its book stays complete.
- Tax Salary does not change: it is fixed at the last Regular Season game
  (CBA2-C08.1). A move before that instant is refused.
- A row belongs to a player through `;player:<id>` on its source reference
  (in-world signings now add this, and trade rows carry it) or the SIMULATION
  baseline id `apron-team-salary:simulation:<team>:minimum-uplift:<id>`.
  Dead-money rows stay with the team. A team holding an active uplift row that
  names no player (an in-world Minimum signing made before this change) cannot
  send players until it is tied to one.
- Proof: unit tests for attribution, the move, a swap and validation; and a
  local browser test where Justin Edwards (PHI, $247,780 uplift) goes to CHA
  for Sion James and back, with each team's Apron Team Salary matching the
  elected amounts and Tax Salary unchanged.

## November 12: taking minimum players with the Minimum Exception (V1 item B1, part 1)

A team may acquire a player on a Minimum Contract by trade using the Minimum
Exception without sending any player back (CBA2-C13.4, C13.6). The new governed
salary path `MINIMUM_EXCEPTION` passes only when:

- the team sends no players and uses no held Traded Player Exception;
- every incoming player's governed salary basis is marked
  `minimumExceptionAcquisition`: the ledger says it was signed using a
  Minimum exception, it covers one or two seasons, it has no trade bonus or
  incentives, and it is not a two-way contract. The salary amount itself is
  not re-checked against the minimum scale; the signing record is the
  evidence.

Matched incoming salary is the actual salary (no matching is required). The
apron restriction table treats this path like ROOM: not applicable unless cash
is paid or the trade includes a sign-and-trade. The CBA2-C07.3 uplift still
moves with the player (B1 prerequisite 3).

Proof: unit tests in `tests/trade/tradeSalaryMatchingPaths.test.ts` and
`tests/trade/governedTradeSalaryBasis.test.ts`; local emulator test
`tests/e2e/architect-simulation-minimum-exception-trade.spec.ts` (CLE sends
Nae'Qwan Tomlin to CHI; CHI elects the Minimum Exception, Apron totals
reconcile and CHI's hard-cap ledger does not change).

Correction (November 12): when this first merged, the browser proof passed
through an older per-player "FA Exception" shortcut that the validator chose
by itself, so CHI's elected path was never evaluated. The next section closes
that shortcut, and the same browser test now runs the Minimum Exception path.

Not yet modelled (later B1 work): a team that sends players and also takes a
minimum player at $0 in the same trade; the CBA-A10.2 aggregation limit on
players acquired with an exception; the Non-Taxpayer, Bi-annual and Room
Mid-Level exceptions in trades (needs daily proration from January 10).

## November 12: Mid-Level and Bi-annual Exceptions in trades (V1 item B1, part 2)

A team that sends no players may take players into one of its free-agent
exceptions. The new governed salary paths are `NON_TAXPAYER_MLE`,
`BI_ANNUAL_EXCEPTION` and `ROOM_MLE` (the Taxpayer MLE is signing-only).

Validation (`tradeSalaryMatchingPaths.ts`) passes only when all of these hold:

- no player is sent, no held TPE is used, and at least one player comes in;
- the trade date is before January 10 of the Salary Cap Year. From January 10
  these exceptions prorate daily; that proration is not modelled, so the
  exception cannot be used from then on (the same limit governed signings
  already have);
- the team's exception record is this Season's official amount for that
  exception (a record holding another Mid-Level flavor's amount is refused),
  it is enabled, and incoming Salary fits its remaining balance;
- one Mid-Level flavor per Season, and the Room MLE and BAE exclude each other
  (the same exclusivity the signing rules apply); the BAE is not used in
  consecutive Seasons;
- the Non-Taxpayer MLE and BAE need Team Salary at the Cap or below it by
  less than the exception amount, and must leave Apron Team Salary at or below
  the First Apron;
- each incoming Contract has no more Seasons left than the exception allows
  (4, 2 and 3). SIMULATION assumption: the signing term limit is applied to an
  acquired Contract. It can refuse a legal trade, never allow an illegal one.

Apply (`applyTradeExceptionAcquisitions`) consumes the incoming Salary from the
exception, records the BAE Season, and for the Non-Taxpayer MLE and BAE sets
the same First Apron hard cap a signing with that exception sets.

The per-player "FA Exception" bucket shortcut is closed in governed trades: it
is no longer auto-selected, choosing it is refused, and players it marked are
matched by the team's elected path like any other player. It skipped every
rule above and consumed nothing at Apply.

Proof: unit tests in `tests/trade/tradeSalaryMatchingPaths.test.ts` and
`tests/trade/tradeExceptionAcquisition.test.ts`; the browser test
`architect-simulation-minimum-exception-trade.spec.ts` now first elects CHI's
Non-Taxpayer MLE on June 5 and checks the January 10 refusal (Apply stays
disabled), then completes the Minimum Exception trade through the governed
path. The four other SIMULATION browser tests still pass.

Not covered here: a trade on or after January 10 (needs the proration rule),
the CBA-A10.2 aggregation wait for exception-acquired players, and the
correctness of exception records after a Season rollover (the rollover keeps
each exception's enabled flag from the year before; that belongs to C1).

## November 12: next season's Apron after the Regular Season (V1 item B2, part 1)

From the day after the last Regular Season game to June 30, a trade that
triggers Apron restriction rows F, H or I (aggregation, TPE use, cash) must also
keep next season's Apron Team Salary at or below that Apron (CBA2-A05.15).
Every June 5 SIMULATION trade is in that window. Before this change, cash
refused it outright and aggregation and TPE trades skipped the second test.

- `subsequentYearApronTeamSalary.ts` computes next season's Apron Team Salary
  after the trade under CBA2-A05.17: options exercised, no ETOs, no further
  transactions, signed extensions counted, the Higher Max assumed for a
  final-season Rookie Scale player with a maximum extension, next-season dead
  money, unlikely bonuses added, and a carried Minimum uplift bounded by next
  season's two-YOS minimum. Free Agent Amounts, unsigned First Round Picks,
  exception amounts and incomplete-roster charges are excluded. A Contract
  ending this season with no recorded extension pays nothing next season.
  Anything it cannot read (a missing next-season Salary, a duplicate row, a
  nonzero trade bonus, a pending Offer Sheet) blocks the trade.
- `tradeApronRestrictions.ts` runs the second test and names the season,
  amount and overage when it fails. The trade passes only when both seasons
  pass.
- Apply records a second hard-cap entry for next season (CBA2-A05.16) at next
  season's official Apron Level, or at this season's Level, labeled, when next
  season's is not published. `tradeHardCapLedgerAuthority.ts` authenticates
  its shape (rows F, H and I only, one year after a post-season transaction).
  `hardCapValidation.ts` holds later trades this season to it.
- SIMULATION books now carry roster-count evidence (no 2026 first-round pick is
  drafted before June 5). Without it a two-for-one trade left a 14-player
  roster on the legacy path, which blocked Apply for a reason unrelated to
  the trade.

Proof: `tests/trade/subsequentYearApron.test.ts` (computation, pass, refusal,
missing inputs, in-season exemption, entry authentication and tampering,
next-season enforcement, the post-trade roster that Apply re-validates) and
updated apron and cash tests. The browser test
`architect-simulation-aggregated-trade.spec.ts` applies CHI's two-for-one for
Grant Williams on June 5 and checks both hard-cap entries (2025-26 Row H at
$207,824,000 and 2026-27 at the official $221,686,000), then reloads; and it
checks that SAC's two-for-one for Josh Hart is refused because SAC's 2026-27
Apron Team Salary of $208,592,345 would exceed the Second Apron by $768,345.

Limits: rows E, G and J are not modelled. Next-season hard caps that real teams
took on from April to June 2026, before the world date, are not in the A3 data.
Cash in player-and-pick trades (the rest of B2) follows.

## November 12: cash with players and picks (V1 item B2, part 2)

- SIMULATION books now start each Team with an empty governed cash ledger

## November 12: the Expanded TPE and the below-cap election (V1 item B3)

New governed salary path `EXPANDED_TPE` (`tradeSalaryMatchingPaths.ts`):

- one or more Traded Players for one or more Replacement Players, in one
  simultaneous trade (CBA2-A02.7);
- incoming Salary may not exceed max(min(2O + K, O + A), 1.25O + K), with O
  the exact aggregate pre-trade Salary, K the $250,000 allowance (zero above
  the First Apron, CBA2-A02.12) and A = $7,500,000 x current Salary Cap /
  2023-24 Salary Cap, computed unrounded from the governed registry
  (CBA2-A02.8). For 2025-26 A is about $8,527,000 and for 2026-27 about
  $9,095,709;
- it is also the below-cap election: a Team under the Salary Cap may elect it
  in lieu of the room path (CBA2-A02.11). Standard and Aggregated TPEs stay
  unavailable below the Cap, and the refusal now says to use room or the
  Expanded TPE.

Row E (CBA2-A05.7) attaches to it: post-trade Apron Team Salary must be at or
below the First Apron, and the trade hard-caps the Team there. After the
Regular Season it also runs the next-season test and records the next-season
hard cap, like rows F, H and I. The ledger schema and its authentication
accept row E entries in both forms.

Proof: Expanded formula tests at both crossovers, at the limit and one cent
above, the below-cap election, the allowance above the First Apron and
missing inputs (`tests/trade/tradeSalaryMatchingPaths.test.ts`); row E
classification, equality and one cent above, ledger creation and
authentication, and the June next-season pair
(`tests/trade/tradeApronRestrictions.test.ts`). Browser:
`architect-simulation-aggregated-trade.spec.ts` has CHI send Tre Jones
($8,000,000) for Grant Williams ($13,645,500) on June 5 under the Expanded
TPE (a Standard TPE allows only $8,250,000), then checks the 2025-26 row E
hard cap at $195,945,000 and the 2026-27 one at $209,015,000, and reloads.

## November 12: players with the legacy 2027 conditional and ranked 2029 picks (V1 item B4)

Ordinary standard and Two-Way Contracts could already travel with original
firsts, complete protection programs, the unresolved 2026 swap rights and the
resolved 2028 overlapping selections. Two families still refused them:

- **Legacy 2027 conditional** (`conditional-2027`). Players are now admitted.
  The family's own rules are unchanged: the conditional CHI 2027 first still

  claim result and positions never change; the exchange must still be
  complete (every team gives and receives), and each Contract moves once.
  Compare starts the money comparison after the 2029 allocation event, which
  has no cap snapshot, exactly as it already did for 2028.

Whole-trade legality (salary matching, aprons, roster) stays with the ordinary
validators, as for the other mixed families.
