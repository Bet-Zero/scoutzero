## Complete-population method

Before editing, the exact literal predicate-template criterion was applied to
the baseline Canon. It enumerated 614 unique case identities across 81
families, exactly matching renewed R9; `CBA2-SC-072` contained zero affected
cases. The inventory and generation/audit helpers remained outside the
repository.

Each enumerated case was then reviewed against its existing LEAF boundary,
input contract, dependency snapshot, evidence chain, and comparison role. Its
input and oracle cells were replaced with:

- a fully assigned positive record and a byte-identical adverse record with
  explicit case-specific overrides;
- concrete dates, time zones, seasons, amounts, operands, thresholds, player/
  Team/Contract states, events, and external records where the LEAF consumes
  them;
- exact calculations, dates, state transitions, lifecycle mutations, or
  permitted/prohibited actions, with PASS/FAIL only as the summary; and
- explicit accepted dependency result IDs/versions when the exercised LEAF is
  a staged aggregate rather than the direct owner of those results.

The complete post-edit audit rejects the former literal, semantically
equivalent predicate-only constructions, unexpanded placeholders, and an
exercised LEAF's ultimate legal status/action as an asserted input. Proposed
amounts or schedules remain inputs only when the LEAF's comparison role is to
validate that concrete proposal against an independently shown calculation.
