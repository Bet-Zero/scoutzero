## Frozen balanced authority

  headers, vocabularies, typed references and joins, direct-current
  endpoints, structured AMEND lineage, source identities/locators, rooted

  silent deletion within those declared structures.

  reasonableness of atomicity/ownership/coverage decisions, evidence

  separation.

  semantic perfection, universal completeness, legal persuasiveness,
  source truth from locator wording, or that no relevant authority exists
  outside the recorded searches.

This division is frozen for R3.1–R9 unless the owner changes the product

## Governing contract corrections

R2.11 changes the canon, repair plan, validator, and this new receipt only.

1. Acceptance receipt rows now carry the acceptance commit they are
   required to match. Duplicate matching rows reject. Structural
   maker/checker fields are grammar-valid and string-distinct; the checker,
   not software, establishes actual independence.
2. DR2 rows have an exact eight-field mechanical minimum. Semantic
   rationale/tiebreak/atomicity/ownership remain review judgments.

   polymorphic for XW2/SXW2 and each member scope joins positionally to the
   corresponding edge scope.
4. SXW2 and XW2 DISP records share the same normalized-scope join.
5. EV2 validation computes complete rooted acyclic closures under the
   declared class matrix and reconciles every LEAF evidence list.
6. Source-date validation checks declared structure only; source support is
   independently reviewed.
7. AMEND detail rows make prior/current lineage mechanically explicit
   across the governed populations. Free-prose mentions no longer satisfy
   preservation.
8. G15R reports actual touched/required populations and controls. Optional
   absent populations are honest, not failures.
9. The governed inventory remains the authoritative data dictionary; its
   earlier universal-algorithm claim is retired.
10. R3.1 and every later maker unit through R8 has an intervening
    independent checker. R8 is canon/register reconciliation only. R9 is
    read-only at a clean topic-branch checkpoint; Phase 2 requires both R9
    ACCEPT and explicit owner acceptance.
