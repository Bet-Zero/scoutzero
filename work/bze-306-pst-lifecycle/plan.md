## Primary classifications (fixed before counting)

Precedence is missing, conflicting, source-partial/uncertain, recovered, complete.
Each exposed ID receives exactly one class. Recovery annotations are orthogonal.

1. **Missing:** no defensible source mapping to the exposed asset/right.
2. **Conflicting:** incompatible assertions about the same branch/as-of state
   cannot be reconciled from the retained evidence. Alternative branches and
   different dated states are not contradictions.
3. **Source-partial/uncertain:** source maps to the asset but lacks governing
   terms/history or explicitly leaves a required assertion uncertain. A fully
   described future alternative is not incomplete merely because its outcome
   is unknown. A blank narrative is not proof of historical completeness.
4. **Recoverable/model loss fixed by the new parser:** source reconstruction is
   complete and restores a demonstrated loss in the comparison model.
5. **Source-complete lifecycle:** source reconstruction is complete without a
   demonstrated comparison-model loss requiring the recovered class.

These are PST content classifications only. Positive-path authority readiness
is separately unavailable throughout this lane. Unresolved implementation
defects block freeze; they cannot be counted as external-source gaps.
